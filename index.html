Angze Li李昂泽

Ph.D. in Communication

Faculty of Social Sciences, University of Macau

I am a researcher in digital journalism and media transformation, with a focus on journalistic role performance, media transformation, and social media influencers. My doctoral research examined CCTV journo-influencers' performativity in the digital era, exploring the intersection of personal, professional, and political performances on social media platforms.

My work employs qualitative methods to investigate how journalistic practices evolve in China's changing media landscape, engaging with critical media studies, platform studies, and cross-cultural communication.
